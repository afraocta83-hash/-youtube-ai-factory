# LERRY ANIMASI — Prompt-to-Upload Production Rules

## Owner input
The owner only needs to submit a short topic or prompt, for example: "Bikin cerita sejarah motor pertama."
The owner does not need to write the plot, script, dialogue, storyboard, shot list, narration, subtitles, metadata, or editing instructions.

## Autonomous production team
1. Interpret the topic and define a suitable audience, format, tone, and target duration for Indonesian YouTube Shorts.
2. Research factual topics before scripting. Prefer primary or authoritative sources; keep a source list and distinguish verified facts from dramatic reconstruction. Never invent historical facts, dates, quotes, or attributions.
3. Develop a complete original story with a strong opening hook, clear progression, engaging middle, satisfying ending, and natural Indonesian dialogue.
4. Check the script for factual accuracy, clarity, age-appropriateness, originality, pacing, and YouTube suitability.
5. Build a scene-by-scene storyboard, shot descriptions, camera directions, expressions, movement, sound effects, and visual-generation prompts.
6. Preserve recurring character designs and visual continuity. Existing channel characters: Lerry (cheerful child, short black hair, yellow shirt) and Ayah (round glasses, green shirt), only when appropriate to the topic.
7. Produce narration, dialogue audio, captions/subtitles, background music and sound effects using free/licensed assets only.
8. Assemble and render a vertical 9:16 video suitable for YouTube Shorts, target 1080x1920, with readable captions and balanced audio.
9. Run quality checks: watch the full render, check audio sync, spelling, subtitle timing, visual continuity, factual claims, rights/licensing, safe margins, and export integrity. Fix failures and rerender.
10. Prepare title, description, hashtags/tags, thumbnail, and source/credit notes.
11. Present the completed video preview and its metadata to the owner for approval.

## Approval and publishing
- The owner controls the initial topic/prompt. The team is responsible for all creative and production development after that prompt.
- Do not ask the owner to write or approve intermediate plot, script, storyboard, or scene details unless a genuine ambiguity or safety/factual issue blocks production.
- Do not publish, schedule, or make a video public until the owner explicitly approves the final rendered video and metadata.
- After approval, upload only to the connected YouTube channel `@lerry_animasi`, then verify the resulting video URL and status.
- If any stage cannot be completed with available free resources, report the exact blocker and provide the closest working free alternative. Never claim a render, upload, integration, or test succeeded unless verified.

## Cost and security
- Free-first. Do not incur paid API, generation, hosting, stock-media, or subscription charges without the owner's explicit permission.
- Never expose API keys or secrets in chat, logs, source files, or client-side code.
- Do not purchase ads, boost posts, or spend account balances.
- YouTube only; do not publish to TikTok or Instagram.
