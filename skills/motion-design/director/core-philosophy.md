# Core Philosophy — Lerry

## Three pillars
1. Emotional intent: define the feeling before selecting animation properties.
2. Visual narrative: every scene has setup, action and resolution.
3. Motion craft: use easing, arcs, anticipation, follow-through and secondary motion.

## Motion layers
- Primary: the mascot's key action.
- Secondary: hands, props, clothing or shadow reacting after the hero.
- Ambient: subtle background life at low amplitude.

## Attention budget
One hero motion per scene moment. Keep secondary movement restrained so narration and captions remain readable. Break long travel into key poses at intervals no larger than roughly one-third of the frame.
