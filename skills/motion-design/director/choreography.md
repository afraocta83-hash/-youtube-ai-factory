# Choreography — Lerry

1. Establish the scene before the hero action.
2. Lead with the character's main readable action.
3. Let props and secondary body parts react slightly after the primary movement.
4. Use background parallax at a fraction of foreground speed.
5. Settle before a cut; do not fade the entire frame to black as a generic transition.
6. Keep all motion frame-deterministic in Remotion.
